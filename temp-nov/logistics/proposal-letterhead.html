1. Executive Summary & Multi-Tier Architecture

To modernize commercial haulage in Nigeria, eliminate freight capital gridlock, integrate statutory risk mitigation, and structure protected broker/consultant networks, this platform is engineered as an enterprise-grade multi-tier digital ecosystem purpose-built for AMS Haulage and Logistics:

  • The Contractor & Freight Dispatch Hub: Enables verified cargo owners (Contractors) to post structured freight manifests with live capacity countdown decrementing logic, automated 24-hour Expression of Interest (EOI) expiration timers, and integrated Goods-in-Transit (GIT) policy procurement.
  • The Transporter Fleet Vault & Gate-In Countdown Engine: Comprehensive vehicle profiling with mandatory truck video verification proofs, and a strict 48-Hour Depletion Clock that revokes job allocations if trucks fail to gate-in at loading depots on time.
  • The Secure In-App Chat & Anti-Circumvention Engine (Disintermediation Shield): Real-time in-app communication between truckers and contractors for operational coordination (dock loading status, ETA, directions) fortified with an automated contact-redaction filter that blocks phone numbers, emails, bank accounts, and off-platform dealing to guarantee 100% platform retention.
  • The Accredited GIT Insurance Underwriters Desk: Direct integration of licensed Goods-in-Transit (GIT) insurance companies into the freight lifecycle, enabling instant digital policy underwriting, automated per-trip premium deductions, and streamlined electronic claims verification.
  • The Bank & Institutional Financing (Factoring) Desk: A secure financial underwriting portal where accredited banks review verified loading and offload waybills directly to disburse milestone freight financing and execute automated multi-way split deductions.
  • The Consultant Onboarding & Quota Desk: A closed-loop referral and licensing engine where authorized freight consultants pay an onboarding fee, operate under dynamic client quota caps, and earn automated commission splits.
  • The Heavy Equipment Leasing Engine: A full 14-step industrial machinery charter workflow (cranes, excavators, forklifts, lowbeds, barges) with inspection video archives, lease agreements, and caution deposit escrow.
  • The Automobile & Heavy-Duty Spare Parts Marketplace: A dedicated B2B e-commerce catalog for prime movers, flatbeds, tippers, and genuine truck components backed by inspection-based escrow settlement.

Asset video verification and automated allocation revocation countdown engine.

3. Architectural Security Guarantees & Statutory Tax Architecture (VAT & WHT)
🛡️ System Security & Data Integrity Guarantees
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict firewall partitioning between Contractors, Transporters, Consultants, Bank Underwriters, and Super Admins. Zero cross-visibility into private margins or bids.
  • Bank Factoring Token Security: Scoped single-use cryptographically hashed tokens with 5-day state machine expiration (max 1 resend) for bank underwriters to audit waybills without master DB access.
  • Mandatory 2FA OTP Authentication: High-risk operations (payouts, bank details change, gate-in verification) require SMS/Email OTP (Termii engine).
  • Private Media & Document Vault: Truck video proofs and waybills are stored in private cloud buckets with time-limited presigned URLs—never indexed on the open web.
  • Immutable Compliance Audit Trail: Every action, countdown expiry, and status change records immutable logs with User ID, timestamp, and IP for NDPC/SCUML auditability.
  • Payment Webhook Hardening: Paystack/Moniepoint webhooks use HMAC SHA-512 signatures to prevent replay attacks or fraudulent payment notifications.
⚖️ Statutory Financial System Architecture (VAT & WHT)
Tax Liability Rules (Per Transaction):
• VAT (7.5%): Paid by 1 entity (Contractor) on the platform service fee.
• WHT (5% or 10%): Deducted from 2 entities (Transporter & Consultant) at source.
• AMS Haulage: Withholding agent accumulating funds for monthly FIRS/SIRS remittance.
  • Automated Withholding Tax (WHT): 5% WHT automatically deducted on haulage trip payouts & consultant commission; 10% WHT on equipment leases.
  • Statutory Holding Sub-Ledger: Deducted WHT accumulates in an administrative tax holding sub-ledger with auto-generated electronic credit advices for beneficiaries.
  • Automated Value Added Tax (VAT - 7.5%): Standard 7.5% VAT is calculated on platform brokerage charges, itemized clearly on all generated manifests and invoices.
  • Itemized Transparent Breakdowns: Invoices show Gross Freight → Transporter Net (Loading Advance + e-POD Balance) → Consultant Cut (Net of WHT) → Platform Fee → VAT → WHT.
  • One-Click Statutory Tax Filing Reports: One-click export of monthly CSV/Excel schedules formatted for seamless submission to the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) and State Revenue Services.
