1. Executive Summary & Problem Diagnosis

The Party Rental Station (TPRS) operates high-value event assets across Nigeria—including 5-ton to 10-ton industrial mobile air conditioning units, heavy generators, marquee structures, chillers, and luxury banqueting equipment. In rapid, event-driven environments, manual spreadsheets, WhatsApp messaging, and fragmented paper slips result in severe operational leaks:

  • Double-Bookings & Scheduling Clashes: High-demand AC units booked for conflicting weekend dates without transit or teardown buffers.
  • Missing Accessories & Cable Theft: Industrial 3-phase cabling, ducting, and power distribution units (PDU) lost at venues with zero staff accountability.
  • Fleet Logistics Blind Spots: Unmonitored truck fuel costs, expired roadworthiness documentation, and delayed site setups.
  • Gatehouse Security Gaps: Equipment leaving central depots without cryptographically verified job manifests.
  • Disputed Customer Damage Claims: Clients refusing damage liability due to lack of timestamped photos and digital handover signatures.

The Solution: Sales Cowboy will engineer a centralized Operations, Fleet & Inventory ERP that enforces TPRS’s Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) across all departments, establishing complete operational transparency and real-time asset control.

2. Core Functional Modules

Module 1: Time-Windowed Asset & Inventory Vault

  • Conflict-Free Reservation Engine: Automatically calculates real-time asset availability factoring in setup windows, transit buffers, and maintenance intervals, completely eliminating double-bookings.
  • Categorized Asset Registry: Digital profiles tracking equipment across live condition states (AVAILABLE, BOOKED, IN_TRANSIT, ON_SITE, MAINTENANCE, QUARANTINE) with dedicated tracking for cooling systems and generators.
  • Warehouse Issue & Return Notes: Barcode/QR-ready digital dispatch scanning with mandatory post-event return inspection checklists and automated variance reconciliation.

Module 2: Fleet Management & Logistics Desk

  • Vehicle Master Register: Centralized compliance vault tracking roadworthiness, hackney permits, and insurance with automated 30-day and 7-day expiration alerts.
  • Trip Sheets & Dispatch Control: Driver assignments, pre-trip odometer and fuel-level logging, and live status tracking from depot to venue.
  • Maintenance Workflow: Structured driver incident reporting, digital vendor quote uploads, and multi-tier management sign-offs.

Module 3: Event Operations & Site Delivery Engine

  • Digital Job Cards: Mobile-friendly manifests linking venue constraints, power requirements, and assigned technician crews to every booking.
  • Pre-Dispatch & Setup Checklists: Mandatory pre-loading testing protocols for high-capacity ACs (compressor drawdown, airflow temperature, oil levels).
  • Mobile Customer Handover & Digital Signatures: On-site digital signature capture from event organizers upon delivery and collection, generating instant legal handover receipts.

Module 4: Incident, Loss & Security Audit Vault

  • Gatehouse Control Desk: Tablet portal for perimeter security; no truck or equipment exits without a verified digital Job Card.
  • Damage & Missing Items Registry: Mandatory high-res photo capture for damaged equipment with automatic repair cost estimates and liability tagging.
  • Segregated Approval Matrix: Granular approval thresholds for field supervisors (≤₦20k), operations managers (≤₦100k), and executive directors (>₦100k).

Module 5: COO Executive Intelligence & KPI Reporting

  • Real-Time Operations Dashboard: Tracks Fleet Availability (≥95%), Inventory Accuracy (≥98%), and On-Time Setup Rates.
  • Cost-Center Analytics: Aggregates fuel spend, maintenance overhead, and asset utilization rates per cooling unit against monthly budgets.
  • One-Click Reporting: Instant export of PDF and Excel operational audit reports for executive board meetings.
