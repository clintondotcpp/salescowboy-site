1. Scope of Services & Deliverables (Web App Only)

1.1 The Developer shall design, build, and deploy the Platform strictly as a responsive, high-SEO Web Application & Progressive Web App (PWA), encompassing five core functional modules:

  • Module 1: High-SEO Public Marketplace & Granular Location Engine: Programmatic category and location routing covering all 36 States, FCT, and 774 Local Government Areas (LGAs) of Nigeria (e.g., "Solar Installer in Owerri Municipal", "AC Repair in Ikeja"), smart search, filter bar by price, verification status, and rating.
  • Module 2: Service Provider Profiles & Portfolio Showcase: Rich service listings with before-and-after photo galleries (Cloudflare R2), call-out/starting rates, operating hours, verified KYC badges, and two-way verified reviews.
  • Module 3: Instant Lead Generation & Contact Suite: "Reveal Phone Number" with click-logging analytics counter, one-click pre-filled WhatsApp inquiry button, and real-time in-app quote request messaging.
  • Module 4: Platform Monetization Engine (Ad Boosting & Subscriptions): Paid profile boosting packages (VIP Gold, Silver, Featured Carousel, Urgent Tag), recurring pro subscriptions, and automated checkout via Paystack (Cards, USSD, Bank Transfer).
  • Module 5: Administrator & Moderation Desk: Admin dashboard to moderate incoming listings/photos, verify submitted KYC documents, manage category taxonomy, configure ad boost rates, and review lead analytics.
