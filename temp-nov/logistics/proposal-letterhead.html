1. Executive Summary & Multi-Tier Architecture

To modernize commercial haulage in Nigeria, eliminate freight capital gridlock, and structure protected broker/consultant networks, this platform is engineered as an enterprise-grade multi-tier digital ecosystem purpose-built for AMS Haulage and Logistics:

  • The Contractor & Freight Dispatch Hub: Enables verified cargo owners (Contractors) to post structured freight manifests with live capacity countdown decrementing logic and automated 24-hour Expression of Interest (EOI) expiration timers.
  • The Transporter Fleet Vault & Gate-In Countdown Engine: Comprehensive vehicle profiling with mandatory truck video verification proofs, and a strict 48-Hour Depletion Clock that revokes job allocations if trucks fail to gate-in at loading depots on time.
  • The Bank & Institutional Financing (Factoring) Desk: A secure financial underwriting portal where accredited banks review verified loading and offload waybills directly to disburse milestone freight financing and execute automated 3-way split deductions.
  • The Consultant Onboarding & Quota Desk: A closed-loop referral and licensing engine where authorized freight consultants pay an onboarding fee, operate under dynamic client quota caps, and earn automated commission splits.
  • The Heavy Equipment Leasing Engine: A full 14-step industrial machinery charter workflow (cranes, excavators, forklifts, lowbeds, barges) with inspection video archives, lease agreements, and caution deposit escrow.
  • The Automobile & Heavy-Duty Spare Parts Marketplace: A dedicated B2B e-commerce catalog for prime movers, flatbeds, tippers, and genuine truck components backed by inspection-based escrow settlement.
2. Detailed Module Breakdown
Module 1: Consultant Onboarding & Quota Desk Licensing & Quotas
Monetizes consultant network access and enforces closed-loop broker referral chains.
  • Paid Consultant Licensing: Mandatory activation fee via Paystack/Moniepoint to unlock consultant dashboard and referral links.
  • Closed-Loop Referral Engine: Contractors and Transporters can register only via an authorized Consultant's invitation link.
  • Account Cap Enforcement: Admin-configured quotas limiting how many active Contractors and Transporters a single consultant can manage.
  • Automated Commission Engine: Automatically routes consultant splits on completed trips, leases, and vehicle sales to their wallet.
Module 2: Contractor & Job Dispatch Hub Dispatch Engine
Structured cargo posting with live capacity decrementing and expression-of-interest expiration.
  • Structured Haulage Manifests: Post cargo briefs detailing consignment type, geofenced loading/offloading coordinates, vehicle configuration, tonnage, trip rate, and mandatory GIT insurance.
  • Capacity Decrementing Logic: Remaining capacity counts down in real time as transporters commit trucks (e.g., Job requires 20 flatbeds → Transporter A commits 5 → 15 remaining).
  • 24-Hour EOI Timer: Transporter expressions of interest automatically expire and release truck holds if not approved within 24 hours.
Module 3: Transporter Fleet Vault & 48-Hour Gate-In Clock Fleet Verification
Asset video verification and automated allocation revocation countdown.
  • Fleet Asset Onboarding: Vehicle profile logging plate numbers, truck video proof uploads, driver licenses, proofs of ownership, and bank details.
  • The 48-Hour Depletion Clock: Acceptance triggers a 48-hour countdown. Failure to gate-in and verify at loading depot automatically revokes the allocation.
  • Dual-Stage Waybill Verification: Stage 1 (Loading Waybill) triggers advance payout; Stage 2 (Proof of Offloading / e-POD) triggers final 3-way settlement.
Module 4: Bank & Institutional Financing (Factoring) Desk Factoring & Underwriting
Direct financial underwriting integration for freight liquidity and automated loan recovery.
  • Contractor-to-Bank Invitation: Contractors invite accredited financial institutions to finance their freight pipeline with a 5-day expiry state machine.
  • Financier Disbursement Desk: Banks inspect approved loading and offload waybill documents directly to authorize milestone disbursements.
  • Automated 3-Way Split Deductions: Dynamically splits financing into Transporter Payment, Platform Service Charge, and Consultant Commission.
Module 5: Heavy Equipment Leasing Engine Industrial Machinery
Full 14-step workflow tailored for industrial plant, machinery, and maritime barges.
  • Workflow Duplication: Tailored for cranes, excavators, forklifts, lowbeds, and barges with 360-degree video inspection archives.
  • Contracts & Escrow: Automated lease contract generation, caution deposit escrow holding, and scheduled maintenance sign-offs.
Module 6: Automobile & Heavy-Duty E-Commerce Marketplace Commerce & Escrow
B2B marketplace for prime movers, flatbeds, tippers, and genuine truck spare parts.
  • Classified Vehicle & Parts Catalog: Comprehensive storefront for verified trucks and components.
  • Inspection-Based Escrow: Platform holds buyer funds in escrow until physical delivery inspection is signed off before releasing seller proceeds.
