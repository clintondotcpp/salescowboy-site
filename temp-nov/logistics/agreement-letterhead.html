1. Scope of Work & System Modules

1.1 The Client hereby engages the Developer to design, develop, test, and deploy the Platform across six (6) core functional modules as detailed in Schedule A:

  • Module 1: Consultant Onboarding & Quota Desk: Paid consultant licensing with Paystack/Moniepoint activation checkout, closed-loop referral system (contractors and transporters register exclusively via unique consultant invite links), dynamic quota caps, and automated commission routing on trips, leases, and vehicle sales into consultant wallets.
  • Module 2: Contractor & Job Dispatch Hub: Structured haulage manifests with geofenced loading/offloading coordinates, vehicle specifications, tonnage, and GIT insurance verification; real-time capacity decrementing countdown logic as transporters commit trucks; and automated 24-hour Expression of Interest (EOI) expiration timers.
  • Module 3: Transporter Fleet Vault & 48-Hour Gate-In Clock: Fleet asset onboarding with vehicle plate numbers, mandatory truck walk-around video proof uploads, driver licenses, and bank accounts (NUBAN); automated 48-Hour Depletion Clock revoking job allocations if trucks fail to arrive and verify gate-in at the loading depot; and dual-stage waybill verification (Stage 1 Loading Waybill advance release, Stage 2 Proof of Offloading / e-POD final 3-way split).
  • Module 4: Bank & Institutional Financing (Factoring) Desk: Contractor-to-Bank financing invitations governed by an automated 5-day expiry state machine (strictly capped at one resend); secure financier underwriting desk granting accredited banks direct audit access to verified loading and offload waybill documents to authorize milestone disbursements; and automated 3-way split deductions (Transporter Payout, Platform Service Fee, Consultant Commission).
  • Module 5: Heavy Equipment Leasing Engine: Full 14-step haulage workflow tailored for industrial machinery (cranes, excavators, forklifts, lowbeds, barges) incorporating mandatory 360-degree video inspection uploads, lease duration agreements, caution deposit escrow, and maintenance sign-offs.
  • Module 6: Automobile & Heavy-Duty E-Commerce Marketplace: B2B classified catalog for prime movers, flatbeds, tippers, and genuine truck components backed by inspection-based escrow settlement that releases merchant funds only upon formal buyer physical delivery sign-off.
