WHEREAS: The Client desires to retain the Developer to design, engineer, and deploy a three-platform real estate ecosystem, and the Developer possesses the specialized technical expertise to perform such engineering services upon the terms set forth herein.

1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

1.1 The Client hereby engages the Developer to design, develop, test, and deploy the Three-Platform Real Estate Ecosystem—comprising the iOS/Android Mobile Application, the Public SEO Web Marketplace (MLS), the Multi-Role Administrative/Surveyor/Accounting/Audit Web Portals, and Polygon Blockchain Integration (collectively, the "Platform")—in accordance with Schedule A (Technical & Commercial Proposal) attached hereto.

1.2 Any major new feature requested after the execution of this Agreement that falls outside the scope of Schedule A shall be governed by a formal written Change Request (CR) with mutually agreed budget and schedule revisions.

2. Total Development Investment & Payment Schedule

2.1 The total all-inclusive development fee for the Platform is Three Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira Only (₦3,500,000).

2.2 The Client shall remit payment across three contractual milestone stages:

3.1 The Client is solely responsible for funding recurring third-party infrastructure and operational expenses as itemized in Schedule A, including Railway cloud hosting ($10–$20/mo), domain registration, Polygon Mainnet gas sponsorship wallets (POL tokens), KYC/identity verification API credits (Prembly/Smile ID), and Apple ($99/yr) / Google ($25) developer accounts.

4.1 The Client shall own 100% complete, exclusive, and unencumbered Intellectual Property (IP) rights in the Platform. Upon receipt of full and final payment (₦3,500,000), all right, title, and interest in and to the Platform—including all custom source code, frontend and backend architectures, database schemas, API interfaces, Polygon blockchain smart contracts, UI/UX designs, visual assets, documentation, and technical deliverables—shall vest completely and exclusively in the Client.

4.2 The Developer waives and transfers all claims, proprietary rights, and title therein to the Client. Upon final milestone settlement, the Developer shall execute complete handover of all Git source code repositories, master administrative credentials, server deployment scripts, and Polygon deployer private keys directly to the Client, free of any ongoing licensing restrictions, royalties, or developer encumbrances.

5. Regulatory, Accounting & Blockchain Disclaimers

5.1 Enterprise Financial Accounting, Escrow & Statutory Compliance: The Developer shall engineer the Platform with a specialized real estate multi-subledger double-entry accounting engine (chart of accounts covering Assets, Escrow/Trust accounts, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Operating Expenses), automated buyer earnest escrow holding and release workflows, statutory tax calculation and deductions (VAT at 7.5%, WHT at 5%–10% with FIRS/NRS-compliant e-WHT certificates), automated multi-tier commission splitting with NUBAN bank transfer automation, rental dividend yield distribution ledgers for fractional holders, and immutable system-wide audit logs (recording every action, timestamp, and User ID) to support statutory inspections by external government bodies (SCUML/NFIU, NRS/FIRS, NDPC, and SEC Nigeria). However, the Client remains solely responsible for obtaining and maintaining all statutory corporate licenses, operating permits, and timely filing of tax returns with government authorities.

5.2 Automated Title Signatures & Surveyor Valuations: The Platform automates digital signature placement on title document templates and records property valuations submitted by the Client's Estate Surveyors. The Client is solely responsible for the legal validity of underlying property titles, physical land registry filings, and the accuracy of surveyor valuations.

5.3 Search Engine Optimization (SEO): The Developer warrants that the Public Web Marketplace shall be engineered with technical SEO best practices (Server-Side Rendering, dynamic sitemaps, and Schema.org real estate metadata). Exact ranking positions on Google depend on ongoing content and market activity and are not guaranteed as fixed positions.

5.4 Polygon Blockchain Tokenization & Secondary Liquidity Protocol: Real estate is an inherently illiquid physical asset. Instant or rapid liquidation is not an unconditional guarantee and is strictly facilitated through three mechanisms: (a) Secondary Marketplace with Limit Orders (users can post their holdings for sale on the platform's internal bulletin board or order book at their preferred price to willing buyers), (b) Optional Treasury-Assisted Rapid Buyback (instant buyback available exclusively through a Platform Liquidity Facility at an automated 5%–10% liquidation spread / haircut to protect the treasury from bank runs), or (c) Structured Exit Windows (scheduled quarterly or bi-annual liquidity rounds where the platform redeems units from verified buyers). Furthermore, fractional property acquisitions are dependent upon sufficient crowd-pool aggregation (e.g., aggregating enough buyers at ₦10,000 per fraction to fully capitalize an asset such as a ₦350,000,000 property). Transactions confirmed on the Polygon Mainnet are irreversible. The Developer shall not be liable for market liquidity shortfalls or losses resulting from compromised administrative private keys under the Client's custody.

6. Acceptance Testing, Warranty & 12-Month Technical Support

6.1 Upon milestone delivery, the Client shall have seven (7) business days to inspect the deliverables and report any reproducible software bugs deviating from Schedule A.

6.2 12-Month Technical Support & Bug-Fix Maintenance: The Developer shall provide twelve (12) months of post-launch technical support and maintenance commencing from the live production launch date. This support is strictly confined to system monitoring, server uptime surveillance, database integrity checks, and diagnosing and resolving any existing bugs or defects arising within the agreed Schedule A scope. This support expressly excludes the design, engineering, or deployment of new features, architectural scope additions, or third-party add-ons, which shall be treated as new feature requests governed by separate written Change Requests (CRs).

7. Limitation of Liability & Governing Law

7.1 The Developer’s maximum aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client under this Agreement.

7.2 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute not resolved amicably within fourteen (14) days shall be referred to mediation or arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Mediation Act, 2023.
