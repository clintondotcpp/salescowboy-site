Opening a brand-new hotel in Oru, Ahiara Mbaise means you have no broken digital reputation or bad legacy systems to fix. During peak dates, hotel rooms book out at ₦25,000–₦60,000/night. Launching with this checklist ensures 100% direct bookings with zero 20% commission loss to OTAs, automated 24/7 WhatsApp room sales, and Day-1 5-star Google review authority.
