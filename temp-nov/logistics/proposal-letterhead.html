1. Executive Summary & The 4-Tier Logistics Ecosystem

To modernize commercial freight haulage across Nigeria, eliminate cargo diversion risks, and structure transparent commission settlements, this platform is engineered as an integrated four-tier digital ecosystem rather than a fragmented standalone mobile app:

  • 1. The Native Mobile Applications (iOS & Android): Dedicated mobile apps published on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store for Truckers, Fleet Owners, and Field Drivers, featuring live GPS checkpoint logging, trip milestone updates, offline gate-pass scanning, and digital waybill capture.
  • 2. The Contractor & Freight Broker Deal Desks (Web & Mobile): Web and mobile portals empowering Cargo Owners (Contractors) to broadcast freight tenders, and Freight Brokers (Middlemen) to lock in protected brokerage commissions without the risk of direct client-trucker bypassing.
  • 3. The Public Web Portal & Heavy Equipment Marketplace: A high-traffic, SEO-optimized web storefront engineered to rank organically on Google Search for truck rentals, crane/excavator charters, genuine spare parts sales, and nationwide freight quotes.
  • 4. The Master Operations, Escrow & Accounting Control Center: An enterprise-grade administration hub providing real-time visibility over active highway corridors, automated 3-way split escrow payouts, demurrage claims, and statutory tax reporting.
2. The Public Web Marketplace & Organic SEO Advantage

While native mobile apps serve drivers on the highway, corporate cargo owners and heavy-equipment buyers discover services via Google Search. Building a dedicated Public Web Marketplace transforms the platform into an organic customer acquisition engine:

  • Nationwide Haulage & Equipment Discovery: Shippers, construction firms, and logistics coordinators across Nigeria can request freight quotes and browse verified equipment without needing an initial app install.
  • Programmatic Route & Cargo SEO Engine: Every freight corridor and equipment category automatically generates Google-optimized landing pages with Schema.org logistics metadata (e.g., "40ft Flatbed Truck Haulage from Apapa Port to Kano", "Caterpillar Excavator Lease in Port Harcourt", "Heavy-Duty Truck Tyres in Lagos"). This captures high-intent B2B search volume organically without continuous ad spend.
  • Verified Freight Rate Directory & Capacity Feed: A transparent freight index showing standard route rates across key Nigerian economic corridors (Lagos–Ibadan, Apapa–Onitsha, PH–Aba, Abuja–Kaduna), establishing the platform as the definitive authority in Nigerian commercial freight.
3. Comprehensive Module Breakdown
Module 1: 3-Role Haulage Transaction, Dispatch & Bypassing-Protection Desk Core Engine
Facilitates seamless load posting, broker bidding, and driver dispatch while preventing disintermediation.
  • Contractor Portal: Post haulage tenders (cargo specs, 20ft/40ft Flatbed, Box, Tipper, Tanker, tonnage, loading bay coordinates, destination, budget).
  • Broker Deal Desk: Middlemen pick up jobs, assign verified truckers, and lock in protected brokerage markups. Data isolation prevents client-trucker bypassing.
  • Trucker Interface: Drivers review manifests, upload compliance credentials (hackney permit, GIT insurance, roadworthiness), and log status milestones.
Module 2: Milestone Haulage Escrow, Mobilization Fuel Advance & Split Wallet Finance & Escrow
Accommodates Nigerian freight payment dynamics with automated multi-wallet settlements.
  • Milestone Fuel Advance: 60%–70% advance released upon loading verification, weighbridge slip capture, and signed waybill upload; 30%–40% locked in escrow until offloaded.
  • Automated 3-Way Split: Escrow splits payout simultaneously between Trucker Wallet, Middleman Commission Wallet, and Platform Royalty Fee upon verified delivery.
  • Instant Commercial Bank Payouts: Automated NIP bank transfers via Paystack/Moniepoint directly to Nigerian commercial bank accounts (NUBAN) 24/7.
Module 3: Digital Waybill & Electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) Verification
Eliminates paper-waybill loss, delivery disputes, and delayed invoice reconciliations.
  • Mobile Waybill Capture: Drivers photograph stamped waybills, tally sheets, and gate passes with GPS coordinates and timestamps embedded.
  • Consignee Digital Sign-Off: Warehouse managers sign directly on-screen or authenticate receipt via automated SMS/WhatsApp delivery authorization PINs.
Module 4: Logistics Equipment, Spare Parts & Heavy Vehicle Leasing Portal E-Commerce & Leasing
A unified marketplace for buying truck parts and leasing industrial machinery.
  • Spare Parts Storefront: Categorized catalog for tyres, batteries, engine blocks, axles, hydraulic jacks, and tarpaulins with multi-merchant vendor accounts.
  • Equipment & Truck Leasing Engine: Book flatbeds, mobile cranes, forklifts, and excavators on daily, weekly, or monthly charters with caution deposit tracking.
Module 5: GPS Checkpoint Tracking, Route Monitoring & Driver Verification Safety & Fleet
Active highway corridor tracking and driver identity verification to prevent cargo loss.
  • Corridor Route Telemetry: Background GPS pings active truck positions along transit corridors for live map tracking by Contractors and Middlemen.
  • Driver Background Screening: Comprehensive identity verification (NIN, BVN, FRSC Commercial Driver’s License) and vehicle chassis validation.
Module 6: Enterprise Financial Accounting, Demurrage & Statutory Tax Ledger Compliance
Double-entry accounting and automated tax deductions for corporate transport compliance.
  • Double-Entry Ledger: Chart of accounts covering Escrow Liabilities, Fuel Advances, Retainage, Driver Payouts, Brokerage Margins, and Royalties.
  • Demurrage Engine: Automated demurrage computation for loading bay or offloading delays exceeding free grace periods.
  • Statutory Tax (VAT & WHT): Automated 7.5% VAT calculation and 5% Withholding Tax deduction with exportable FIRS/NRS-compliant tax schedules.
Module 7: Master Operations Center & Dispute Resolution Desk Command Center
Complete administrative oversight, dispute resolution, and regulatory audit readiness.
  • Operations Command Hub: Bird’s-eye monitoring of active trips, pending escrow releases, verified driver queues, and lease contracts.
  • Dispute Resolution Desk: Structured arbitration workflow for transit cargo damages, missing goods, or delay claims before escrow disbursement.
  • 100% Immutable Audit Trails: Every system action, login, waybill sign-off, and payout is permanently logged with user ID, GPS, and timestamp.
