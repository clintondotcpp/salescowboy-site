1. Executive Summary & The Unified Super App Vision

Swiftly Go is engineered to become Abia State’s premier on-demand consumer platform. Rather than building fragmented applications for passenger transportation, restaurant ordering, and parcel deliveries, Swiftly Go consolidates these high-frequency utilities into a single, cohesive super app ecosystem.

By anchoring on a shared local mobility fleet (Keke Napep / Tricycles and Dispatch Motorcycles), the platform achieves dual operating leverage:

  • High Daily Frequency & Habit Retention: Driving high app opens through staple daily habits (morning commuting, afternoon office lunch, and commercial market parcel transfers).
  • Zero Driver Idle Time (Cross-Dispatch): The same driver fleet handling peak-hour morning commuter transport is cross-dispatched to service midday restaurant deliveries and afternoon B2B market waybills, maximizing daily driver yield and Gross Merchandise Value (GMV).

3. Core Platform Deliverables (Phase 1 Ecosystem)
1. Customer Super App (iOS & Android) Consumer Tier
Single sign-on interface with seamless switching between three daily habit utilities.
  • Swiftly Rides (Keke Hailing): Upfront transparent distance/zone fares, driver verification, union alignment ratings, and live vehicle approach tracking.
  • Swiftly Food Ordering: Curated restaurant menus, custom food modifiers, live kitchen order status, and temperature-conscious dispatch.
  • Swiftly Send (P2P & B2B Logistics): Intra-city parcel dispatch under 25kg, live route map tracking, digital Proof of Delivery (OTP + recipient photo).
  • Unified In-App Wallet: Fast wallet funding via Paystack/Moniepoint (Cards, USSD, Virtual Accounts) and instant peer-to-peer transfers.
2. Driver & Fleet Partner App (Android-First) Fleet Tier
Built for Nigerian commercial drivers with acoustic alerts and seamless cross-dispatch.
  • Shift Toggle & Demand Radar: Online/Offline availability toggle with audible acoustic trip broadcasts and demand corridor heatmaps.
  • Dynamic Cross-Dispatch: Drivers switch effortlessly between passenger trips, restaurant pickups, and market parcels without app switching.
  • Driver Daily Ledger: Transparent earnings tracker showing trip totals, commission debits, and 24/7 bank withdrawals.
3. Merchant & Restaurant Partner Hub (Web & Tablet) Merchant Tier
Empowers local food vendors and retail stores to manage orders and kitchen operations.
  • Menu & Inventory Desk: Real-time pricing updates, photo uploads, item variation toggles, and stock-out alerts.
  • Kitchen Order Display: Audible order chime, prep time countdown timers, and Bluetooth receipt printer integration.
4. Master Operations, Dispatch & Admin Control Center (Web) Admin Tier
Central command system for monitoring city mobility, dynamic surge pricing, and revenue splits.
  • Live City Heatmap: Real-time spatial tracking of all active Keke tricycles and dispatch bikes across Aba and Umuahia.
  • Geofencing & Corridor Pricing: Polygon mapping to configure operating corridors, base fares, night surge, and union levy fees.
  • KYC Verification Gate: Identity review for drivers (NIN, Driver’s License, union badges) with one-click verification approval.
  • Automated Split Revenue Ledger: Automated accounting tracking platform take-rates (12%–18% Rides, 15%–25% Food, 15%–20% Logistics).
